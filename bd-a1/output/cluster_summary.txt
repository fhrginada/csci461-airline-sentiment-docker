INTERPRETATION
- K-Means is unsupervised: it never used the labels. It grouped tweets by shared vocabulary.
- TF-IDF (stop words removed) + TruncatedSVD(50 components, 17.0% of the variance) + L2 normalisation.
- k was chosen by experiment (k=2..12, inertia, silhouette, smallest-cluster size, reading top terms), not fixed to 3 (the number of classes): silhouette has no clear peak and is low for every k (short, sparse tweets), the elbow is gradual after k~6-8, and k=8 gives clusters with >500 tweets and distinct themes (k=6 merges themes, k=10 splits the positive tweets into near-duplicate clusters).
- The silhouette is low (0.090): clusters overlap and are not well separated, which is normal for short tweets. The clusters are useful for exploring topics but are not sharp.
- Against the real labels: ARI=0.025, NMI=0.066 (both near 0). The clusters follow topic, not sentiment, so cluster IDs do not match class IDs. Some clusters still lean to a label: thank-you clusters are mostly positive; customer-service, gate and cancellation clusters are 75-80% negative.
